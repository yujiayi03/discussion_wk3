Pastries by Jiayi Yu

Sweet Pairings for Coffee and Tea

Hi! I am Jiayi Yu. I created this page to complete our Coffee and Tea site with the perfect companions: fresh bakery pastries. Each pastry below comes from the images/pastries folder and pairs wonderfully with a hot drink.

My top pick is a buttery croissant with a cappuccino, but a cinnamon roll with chai tea is a close second. All photos are displayed with consistent styling from our shared style.css file.

Top Three Reasons I Picked Pastries

  • They pair perfectly with both coffee and tea
  • They make the site feel warm and welcoming
  • They let me practice accessible image styling
